Bloom

Bloom notices the time you spend with friends in person, asks how it felt, and shows you the story of your friendships across the week and the month.

This is a prototype, so it isn't signed by Apple and macOS will stop you the first time you open it. Getting past that takes about twenty seconds, once.

Opening it the first time

  1. Drag Bloom into your Applications folder, then double-click it. macOS will say it can't verify the developer — click Done. Nothing you do here will work yet; that's expected.

  2. Open System Settings and choose Privacy & Security in the sidebar.

  3. Scroll that page all the way down — past Location Services, Contacts, Photos and the rest — until you reach the Security section at the bottom.

  4. Under Security you'll see “Bloom” was blocked to protect your Mac. Click Open Anyway.

  5. Confirm with Open Anyway, then unlock with Touch ID or your password.

Bloom opens. From then on it's an ordinary double-click — you only do this once.

What's real and what isn't

This build never touches your data. It doesn't read your contacts, your location, your calendar or your photos. The permission screens are part of the prototype — tapping Allow grants nothing.

  • Signing up is simulated. No message is sent and nothing is checked. Type any phone number and any code, or just press the arrow.
  • The friends are invented. Nora Patel and Leo Ortiz, along with every hangout, photo and recap you'll see, are made up for this build.
  • The hangout log is pre-written. In a real version Bloom would notice these from your phone; here they're already in place so there's something to look at.

Finding your way around

Bloom runs in a fixed iPhone-sized window. It starts at the beginning, so you'll walk through signing up before you reach the app itself.

  • Home suggests someone to see and asks how your last hangout felt. Answer one and watch the card settle into the list below.
  • Friends is the dial — scroll it sideways, tap a face to open that friend and their history with you.
  • Recaps is the week and the month as bubbles, sized by how often you saw someone. Tap one. Scroll past the top to step back a week.
  • Profile is you, your settings, and the places you've saved.
